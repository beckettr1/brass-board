How It Works

Selling your cards to Brass & Board is simple. Here's exactly what to expect from start to finish.

The Process

1
Check Our Rates
Head to our Pokemon or Magic buy rates page to see what we pay. All prices are based on TCGPlayer lowest verified seller. If you think you have something valuable, reach out and we'll take a look.
2
Reach Out
Contact us by email, Facebook, or Instagram with a list or photos of what you have. The more detail the better -- card names, set, condition, and quantity. We'll get back to you quickly with an offer.
3
We Confirm the Offer
Once we've reviewed your list or photos, we'll send you a confirmed offer. If anything is unclear we may ask for additional photos before finalizing.
4
Ship Your Cards
Pack your cards securely -- penny sleeves and top loaders for singles, a sturdy box for bulk. Ship to the address we provide. We recommend adding tracking for your peace of mind.
5
Get Paid
Once your cards arrive and we verify the condition, we send payment within 24 hours -- usually same day.
Need payment upfront? We offer Venmo Goods & Services. Note: the 1.9% + $0.10 fee is covered by the seller. All other payment methods are sent after cards arrive.
